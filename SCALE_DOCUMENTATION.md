## MODES (7 Modes of the Major Scale)

### 1. Ionian (Major)
- **Notes (from C):** C, D, E, F, G, A, B
- **Intervals:** Root, Major 2nd, Major 3rd, Perfect 4th, Perfect 5th, Major 6th, Major 7th

- **Triads:** Cmaj, Dmin, Emin, Fmaj, Gmaj, Amin, Bdim
- **Chord Progression:** I-IV-V (or I-vi-IV-V for pop)
- **Emotion:** Bright, happy, triumphant, confident, resolute, celebratory, heroic, optimistic, victorious, radiant, joyful, affirming, uplifting, majestic, golden
- **Category:** Mode

## NOTATION GUIDE

### Semitone Pattern Legend
- **W** = Whole step (2 semitones)
- **H** = Half step (1 semitone)
- **W+H** = 3 semitones (minor 3rd)

### Chord Abbreviations
- **maj** = Major triad
- **min** = Minor triad
- **dim** = Diminished triad
- **aug** = Augmented triad
- **maj7** = Major 7th chord
- **min7** = Minor 7th chord
- **7** = Dominant 7th chord

### Roman Numeral Analysis
- **I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII** = Major chords (uppercase)
- **i, ii, iii, iv, v, vi, vii°** = Minor/diminished chords (lowercase)
- **b** = Flat (lowered by half step)
- **#** = Sharp (raised by half step)
